Its history began in 1998, when the Cambodia Youth Volunteer Organization (CYVO) provided training for young Cambodian professionals in business, management, and English. In 2000, it developed into the Faculty of Management and Law (FML), adding areas such as accounting, finance, human-resource management, computer studies, and law. It then became Build Bright University in 2002.
BBU says that it is recognized by Cambodia's Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport. The university provides Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral degree programs.
